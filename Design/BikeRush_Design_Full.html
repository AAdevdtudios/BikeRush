BIKE//RUSH

Working title. An arcade bicycle racer where you shift gears on the ground, shift your weight in the air, and shift worlds through portals mid-race.

Studio Razorbill Studio Engine Unity 6 · URP Platform Web (itch.io) + Windows Deadline 1 Dec 2026, 13:37 PST Status Plan, not started

The pitch

Pedal hard, launch off the ridge, flip it, stick the landing and rip through a portal into another world, then drop back into the race three places ahead.

Feel

Fast, physical and a little dangerous. A clean landing feels like a reward; a bad one throws your rider into the dirt.

Hook

Every big jump is a choice: play safe, go for a trick and boost, or aim for the SHIFT portal that warps you into a challenge world.

Why bikes

Bikes are light enough to leave the ground all the time, so air time and tricks are part of every lap instead of a rare event.

Magenta steps are where the SHIFT theme lives. Boost feeds speed, speed feeds bigger air, bigger air feeds better tricks.

MechanicsTwo skills at the core, one kept light so new players aren't overloaded.

Riding

Built on Simple Bicycle Physics: a real two-wheel rig with scripted lean, so the bike never falls over on its own. Pedal W, brake S, steer and lean A D, hop by holding then releasing Space.

The pack's sprint becomes boost: hold Shift to burn it. Boost only comes from tricks, drafting and close calls, never for free.

Tricks

In the air, steering spins the bike and pedal / brake flips it forward / back. Ctrl held through take-off is a wheelie launch for extra height. Each rotation counts: 180, 360, 540, front flip, back flip, and combos of both.

Trick value = rotation + air time + variety. Repeating the same trick in a row pays less, so players mix it up.

Landing

The moment that decides everything. A ring on the HUD closes as the ground approaches; press Shift when it meets the target.

  • PERFECT: full trick value as boost, plus a speed kick out of the landing.
  • CLEAN: wheels straight, no press. Normal trick value.
  • SKETCHY: off-angle. Half value, you lose speed.
  • BAIL: way off. Ragdoll, a 2-second reset, no boost.

Reuses the SHIFT//RUSH timing diamond, already built and liked in testing.

Chains

Land, then take off again within 3 seconds and the chain multiplier climbs: x2, x3, up to x5. Slipstream, close calls and wall skims from SHIFT//RUSH also count as links.

Gears (light)

Gears shift automatically, with a cadence needle on the speedo. Players who tap Q / E as the needle hits green get a Perfect Shift nudge of speed and a sliver of boost. Optional skill, never required.

Stretch full manual gears with climbs that punish the wrong gear.

SHIFT worldsPortals hang over the biggest jump on each course, one or two per lap.

Fly through the ring with a trick in progress and the world shifts: slow-mo flash, then you land in a 10 to 15 second challenge somewhere else. Finish it in time and you drop back into the race higher up, with boost. Run out of time and you keep a share of the gain, never ending further back than where you left. A leader keeps their lead.

World 1

Neon Pump Track

A glowing BMX track in the dark. Pump the rollers in rhythm, every crest hit on the beat is a speed burst. Teaches flow.

World 2

Rooftop Gaps

City rooftops at sunset, gaps between buildings. Bunny hop and boost to clear them before the timer runs out.

World 3

Trick Arena

A bowl of ramps. Land as many tricks as you can in 12 seconds; the score sets how far you jump ahead.

World 4

Gear Climb

A brutal hill. Only perfect gear shifts get you to the top in time.

Courses for the jam: two. Ridge Line, a mountain road with big jumps (Race, Time Trial), and Pine Drop, a downhill forest trail (Downhill, Time Trial). Bikes ride at 30 to 65 km/h, so courses are 600 m to 1.2 km, about a third of SHIFT//RUSH's tracks.

Tuning sliders

  • Gearing: quick pick-up ↔ higher top speed
  • Tyres: slick and fast ↔ knobbly and grippy
  • Suspension: stiff and responsive ↔ soft and forgiving
  • Frame weight: light and agile in the air ↔ stable at speed

Each maps to a value the bike physics already has (torque, top speed, wheel friction, suspension, mass), so tuning is a real change in feel.

Look

Pick a rider from the pack's riders (MTB riders, girl biker, cyclist), a bike paint and a helmet colour. Small, but it makes the bike yours on day one.

Stretch unlockable paints for trick milestones.

First five minutesWhat a jam judge sees. If this is fun, they keep playing.

  1. Splash, then straight into the menuThe rider idles on a turning plinth. One button: RIDE.
  2. Make your riderName, rider, paint. Three choices, ten seconds, skippable.
  3. First run: Ridge Line, guidedThe coach card teaches pedal, steer, hop, then the first jump and the landing press. No rivals, no timer.
  4. First trick and first portalA gentle ramp with a portal over it. Clear it and you're in the Neon Pump Track. Every first-timer gets to see a SHIFT world.
  5. First race, on CRUISEFive rivals, two laps. Tuned so a new player can win with one good portal.
  6. Win screen with an unlock"KESTREL BMX UNLOCKED" plus the next event. A reason for one more run.

ProgressionLight, so a judge sees something new every few minutes.

Stars

Each event awards up to 3 stars: finish, podium, and a challenge (land 5 perfects, use a portal, beat a time).

Unlocks

Stars unlock the second and third bikes, the downhill course, the Time Trial ghost and tuning parts.

Risks & cutsDecided now, so we never panic-cut later.

Biggest risks

  • AI riders: the pack has no real AI. We adapt SHIFT//RUSH's rival driver; if it fights the bike physics, rivals ride on a simplified rig.
  • Landing feel: the whole game hangs on it. It gets the most playtesting.
  • Download size: SHIFT//RUSH's web build was 185 MB. Cap textures for web from day one.

Cut order if we run late

  • 1. Gear Climb and Trick Arena worlds
  • 2. Manual gears (keep the auto-gear needle)
  • 3. Elimination and Trick Jam events
  • 4. Paint unlocks
  • Never cut: tricks, landing press, one portal world, one race, first five minutes
