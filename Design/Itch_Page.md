## Description

**BIKE//RUSH** is an arcade bicycle racer made for **GitHub Game Off 2026**. The theme is **SHIFT**.

Pedal down dirt trails against five rivals. Hit a jump and time slows down. A diamond closes in. Press **Shift** when it turns green and your rider throws a stunt. Land it and you get boost.

### Three ways to ride

- **PACK RACE**: you against five rivals. Draft, grab boost pads, be first over the line.
- **GHOST RUN**: an empty trail, you and the clock. Race the ghost of your best run.
- **AIR SHOW**: the camera goes side-on over a stunt course. Fly through the boxes, hit Shift on the diamond, land clean, build a score.

### Three trails

- **Ridge Line**: pine forest and mountain switchbacks.
- **Dust Devil**: a frontier town, a red rock canyon, a stone arch and a trestle drop.
- **Cutlass Cove**: a pirate harbour, a beach wreck, a sea arch and a jungle run.

### Three Air Show venues

- **Cloudbreak**: high above the clouds at sunset.
- **Red Rock**: a rodeo on the mesa at high noon.
- **Cannon Bay**: a headland over the sea with ships sailing past.

### Three difficulties

- **CRUISE**: slower rivals, boost refills fast.
- **RUSH**: rivals push harder, boost refills at half speed.
- **OVERDRIVE**: the fastest rivals, boost refills slowly. You have to earn it with stunts.

First time playing? A short tutorial runs on Cruise until you finish it. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Controls

| Key | Pad | Action |
|---|---|---|
| W | Right trigger | Pedal |
| S | Left trigger | Brake, then reverse |
| A / D | Left stick | Steer and lean |
| Space (hold, then let go) | | Bunny hop |
| Left Shift | | On the ground: boost. In the air: stunt |
| R | | Reset to the trail |
| Esc | | Pause |

You can rebind every control in Settings, Controls.

## Known issues

This is a jam build. These are the problems we know about:

- **Stunts and flips may not look or score right.** The bike levels itself in the air, so a flip can look short, land at an odd angle, or not match the stunt you pressed for.
- **"TOO EARLY" shows up a lot.** The stunt timing window is tight. Wait for the diamond to turn fully green.
- **You can get stuck on props.** Crates, rocks and fences can stop the bike dead. Press R to get back on the trail.
- **Big jumps can reset you.** If you fly far off the trail, the game may put you back on it and you can lose places.
- **The first load is slow.** The game is about 82 MB. Later loads use the browser cache.
- **Desktop browsers only.** There are no touch controls. Chrome and Edge run it best.
- **Balance is not final.** Difficulty and boost refill speeds may change after the jam.

Found something else? Leave a comment with the trail, the mode and what happened.
